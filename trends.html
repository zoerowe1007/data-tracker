How to read this page

Loading the history…

  • Chart explorer (first section): pick items and see each of their readings as a line over the days.
  • What the history shows (second section): five findings. Each has a headline, a plain-words explanation, the exact way the number is computed, a chart and, under "Show the numbers", the table behind the chart.
  • Nothing is typed in by hand: your browser reads data/history.csv and calculates everything, so the page grows smarter as days are added. With only a few days, some findings say they need more history.

Chart explorer

1. Pick items. Tick any coins or places below; the charts under it update straight away.

2. Choose the scale.

Index lets items of very different size share a chart: every line starts at 100, so 110 means "up 10% since the first day". Actual values shows the real numbers (dollars, °F …), best for one item or items of similar size. Percent readings (24h change, humidity) always show their real values. Each chart shows one reading; a chart appears only if a picked item reports it.

What the history shows

Five findings, each worked out from the history file. "In plain words" says what it means; "How it is computed" gives the exact rule.
